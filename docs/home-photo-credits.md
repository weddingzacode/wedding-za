## Fonts

The homepage uses unmodified Latin WOFF2 files supplied by Google Fonts:
Instrument Serif (regular and italic) and Manrope (variable weights 400–800).
Local CSS family aliases avoid competing with the remote font declarations.

The full copyright and SIL Open Font License notices are bundled as
`assets/home-instrument-serif-license.txt` and `assets/home-manrope-license.txt`.
Their upstream sources are the [Instrument Serif license](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/instrumentserif/OFL.txt)
and [Manrope license](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/manrope/OFL.txt).
